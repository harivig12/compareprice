Scheduled adds · Milkbasket

Feature 3. At a set time, the app adds your chosen product to your Milkbasket cart and stops. It never checks out, never edits other cart lines, and skips (and tells you) when it can't add.

References used: guides/mobile-app-flows.md (settings, lists and forms on a phone: rows become cards, one column), guides/dashboards.md (status in words plus glyph, tabular figures, designed empty state), tokens/tokens.css and the app's glass tokens.
  • Each account has its own list (the tabs). One master switch pauses everything.
  • Hard limits apply to every run: max quantity per item, max ₹ per day, listed items only.
  • Results always say a word: Added, Skipped: out of stock, Skipped: signed out, Failed.
