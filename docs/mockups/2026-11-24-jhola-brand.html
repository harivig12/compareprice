Jhola · logo and social image options

Each option is a yellow jhola (the cloth shopping bag) on the app's green. Each one is shown as the home-screen icon, at browser-tab size, in the app's top bar, and on a phone home screen. Pick one, and it will replace the basket icon everywhere: favicon, home-screen icons, top bar, guide, social image and README.

I found no existing Jhola logo in the workspace, so these are new designs. All three stay readable at 16 px and keep the bag inside the safe area for Android's round and squircle icon masks.
