What I took from the Awwwards app sites:
  • One device that stays on screen while its screens change as you scroll.
  • Very large headlines and a scrolling marquee strip.
  • A demo you can try on the page (type "kismis" in E).
  • Tiles that light up under the pointer, and glass panels with a gradient behind them.
All motion stops if your device is set to reduce motion, and every page still works without JavaScript. Text contrast, 44 px touch targets and no sideways scroll at 390 px are kept from round 1.

compareprice

One list.Seven sites.One answer.

Paste your groceries once. compareprice checks Instamart, BigBasket, Zepto, Blinkit, KPN Fresh, JioMart and Flipkart Minutes at your address, and tells you where the basket costs least.

  1. 01

    Open the app

    Start it with docker compose up -d or basket serve, then go to localhost:7777 in your browser.

  2. 02

    Paste your list

    Open Your list and type one item per line. Put 2x in front for a quantity, then tick the sites you want checked.

  3. 03

    Find the cheapest

    You get an answer at once from saved prices, each tagged with its date. Click Get fresh prices to check live, and a chip shows each site's progress.

  4. 04

    Read the answer

    Dark green is the cheapest price for an item and light green is second. Three boxes show the cheapest site, the runner-up and the cheapest mix.

  1. 01

    Open it on Wi-Fi

    Run basket serve --host=0.0.0.0 on the computer. On your phone, open http://<computer-ip>:7777.

  2. 02

    Pin it

    In the browser menu, tap Add to Home screen. After that it opens with one tap, like an app.

  3. 03

    Cards, cheapest first

    On a phone each item becomes a card, with its sites sorted by what you'd pay.

  4. 04

    Sign in with an OTP

    Only Zepto, Milkbasket and Amazon need this. Enter your mobile number, then type the OTP the site sends.
