How to use

Your basket, priced on 7 sites. Before you tap order.

Paste a grocery list once. See which site is cheapest for the whole basket and for every item, with the lowest price each site has ever shown.

  1. 01

    Open the app

    Start the service with docker compose up -d (or basket serve), then open localhost:7777.

  2. 02

    Paste your list

    Open Your list. Type one item per line, like 2x toor dal 1kg, and tick the sites to check.

  3. 03

    Find the cheapest

    Answers straight away from saved prices. Every cell shows its date (as of 22/11/26), and no site is contacted.

  4. 04

    Get fresh prices

    Checks the sites live. A chip per site shows progress. If a site needs a pause, a short countdown runs and the check then starts on its own.

  1. 01

    Join the same Wi-Fi

    Run basket serve --host=0.0.0.0 on the computer, then open http://<computer-ip>:7777 in your phone's browser.

  2. 02

    Add it to your home screen

    In Chrome or Safari, tap Share or the menu, then Add to Home screen. It opens with one tap after that.

  3. 03

    One card per item

    On a phone the table becomes cards. Sites are sorted by what you'd pay, cheapest at the top.

  4. 04

    Sign in with an OTP

    For Zepto, Milkbasket or Amazon, tap Sign in…, enter your mobile number and type the OTP the site sends.
