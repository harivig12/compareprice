How to use compareprice

Groceries, priced everywhere at once.

Write your list once. compareprice checks Instamart, BigBasket, Zepto, Blinkit, KPN Fresh, JioMart and Flipkart Minutes at your address. It tells you where the whole basket costs least, and where each item does. Use it on the computer it runs on, or on your phone over home Wi-Fi.

Start the service

compareprice runs on one computer, which can be a laptop, a desktop or a small home server. It keeps a browser warm for each site, checks your list on a schedule, and serves the page you use. Do this once.

# get the code
git clone https://github.com/harivig12/compareprice.git
cd compareprice/compareprice

With Docker recommended

It stays on and starts again with the machine.

# in the compareprice folder
mkdir -p ~/basket-data
docker compose up -d --build

Without Docker

You need Node 22.13 or newer and Google Chrome. If Chrome isn't on your PATH, set CHROME_PATH.

npm install
node src/cli.js serve

Then open it

In a browser on the same computer:

http://localhost:7777

Settings are at /settings, and this guide is at /guide.

On the website

This is the full view on a computer: a table with items as rows and sites as columns. It works best at 1280 px or wider. With six or more sites, every column still fits without scrolling sideways.

1

Write your list

Open Your list and type one item per line.

  • Put a quantity in front: 2x, 2 x or 2.
  • A pack size such as 500g, 1kg or 1L is optional.
  • Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
2x toor dal 1kg
amul butter 100g
kismis 500g
# weekend
onion 1kg
2

Tick the sites

Pick which sites to compare. Your defaults come from Settings. Sites marked needs login work once you've signed in (step 8).

✓ JioMart✓ KPN✓ BigBasket✓ BlinkitZeptoInstamart
3

Press Find the cheapest

You get an answer at once from saved prices, and no site is contacted. Every price shows when it was saved (as of 22/11/26). An item with nothing saved yet says no saved price.

Saved prices from 21/11/26 – 23/11/26. No site was contacted.
4

Press Get fresh prices

This checks the sites live. Each site gets a progress chip. If a site was searched moments ago, or is resting after a block, the page counts down (wait 3s…) and then starts by itself. Stop cancels the run.

  • Dark green is the cheapest price for that item, and light green is second.
  • Badges compare the price with the lowest each site has ever shown: new low, at lowest, or the low and its date.
  • Greyed cells are shown but not counted. That covers a bigger pack than you asked for, a weak name match (marked ?), or a price far from what other sites charge (price looks wrong, left out).
  • Totals are for goods only. Delivery and handling fees are added only if you set them up (see Good to know).
6

Check a saved price

Start typing in the search box at the top, such as onion or kis, and pick a suggestion. You'll see what each site last charged per 100 g, 100 ml or piece, with dates and the lowest rate seen.

⌕ kis → raisins 500g · 3 sites · 23/11/26
7

See recent runs

Recent runs at the bottom lists the last ten jobs, whether started by you, a schedule or the sweep, with their status and errors. You can follow a scheduled run there as it happens.

8

Sign in, only where needed

Every site works as a guest except Zepto, Milkbasket and Amazon. Press Sign in, pick the site, enter your mobile number and type the OTP it sends. The session stays in the service's browser, so you won't need another OTP until the site signs you out.

9

Set it and forget it

In Settings, set your delivery location, default sites, a default list and schedules. Schedules use 24-hour times, as many per day as you like, and each can have its own sites. Scheduled runs keep the saved prices fresh, so Find the cheapest is always recent.

daily-check07:0009:0012:00

On your phone

It's the same app and the same saved prices, just laid out for a small screen. Each item becomes a card with its sites sorted cheapest first. The two main buttons sit at the bottom, within reach of your thumb. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer running the service.

1

Let the phone reach the service

By default the service only answers on the computer it runs on. To open it to your home network, do one of these:

Without Dockernode src/cli.js serve --host=0.0.0.0
With DockerIn docker-compose.yml, change "127.0.0.1:7777:7777" to "7777:7777", then run docker compose up -d.

If Windows asks about the firewall, allow access on private networks only.

Sign in with an OTP

Tap Sign in at the top. The OTP box takes your phone's autofill, so the code often fills itself in.

Keep it on your home Wi-Fi

The page has no login. Once it listens on 0.0.0.0 or port 7777:7777, anyone on the same network can run comparisons and change your settings. Never forward port 7777 to the internet or use it on public Wi-Fi. To lock it down again, go back to the default 127.0.0.1.

Good to know

A few things that make the results more useful once you know how they work.

One product, many names

The catalogue has about 230 products, each with its English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada names and common misspellings. Any of them is searched and saved under one name, so kismis, kishmish and raisins never split into separate results. Try it:

raisins 500g
Raisins · dry-fruits

Saved vs fresh

Find the cheapest reads saved prices instantly and shows each one's date. Get fresh prices checks the sites and adds what it finds to the history.

Scheduled runs and the sweep keep the saved prices up to date without you doing anything.

Brand or any brand

moong dal matches any brand of moong dal, but never moong dal namkeen. amul butter only matches Amul. A brand you name is never swapped for another.

Catalogue sweep

In the background, the service prices 2–4 catalogue products at a time at random 5–20 minute gaps, starting with ones that have no saved price. That way new searches often have an answer already. You can change it or turn it off in Settings.

Delivery fees

Sites only show their fees at checkout, so totals are for goods only. If you know a site's fees, add them once:

basket fees --set blinkit \
  --delivery 25 --handling 9 \
  --free-above 199

Fix it

When a site fails, the page says why. It names one of these kinds and includes the site's own message, never just "offline".

KindWhat it meansWhat to do
blockedThe site hit a bot wall or rate limit (403 or 429).Wait. The site rests for a while before it's searched again. Keep your schedule to once or twice a day.
sessionThe site's guest session expired, or it wants you to sign in.It refreshes itself and retries once. For Zepto, use Sign in.
unserviceableThe site doesn't deliver to your address.Check the location in Settings, or untick that site.
timeoutThe site took too long to answer.Try Get fresh prices again later.
parseThe site changed what it sends back.This needs a code fix. Note the message and report it.
not_foundThe site answered but has nothing matching.Try a more general name, such as "moong dal" instead of "organic split moong dal".
browserThe headless Chrome or its tab stopped.Run again. If it keeps happening, restart the service.
"Could not start Chrome"

Chrome is installed but the service didn't find it. Set CHROME_PATH to it, for example on Windows: setx CHROME_PATH "C:\Program Files\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe". Then restart the service.

The phone can't open the page

Check that the phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi, that the service listens on 0.0.0.0 (or Docker publishes 7777:7777), and that the firewall allows port 7777 on private networks.

Everything says "no saved price"

Nothing has been checked for those items yet. Press Get fresh prices once, or wait for the next scheduled run or sweep.

A run sits in "queued"

Only one job runs at a time, so it's waiting for the one ahead of it. Recent runs shows what's running, and Stop cancels it.

Did today's scheduled runs happen?

Run node scripts/verify-runs.js. Every finished run is also written to results/runs.jsonl in the data folder.

A price looks wrong

Prices far from what other sites charge are left out automatically and listed under Price check. A cell marked ? is a weak name match, so check the product name in it.

In the terminal

Everything the page does, you can also do from the command line. basket is short for node src/cli.js.

basket search "amul butter"One item across all sites
basket basket.txt --uiCompare a list file in a boxed terminal view
basket "toor dal 1kg" --cachedFrom saved prices only, without contacting any site
basket history "toor dal 1kg"The lowest price each site has shown, with dates
basket catalog "kismis"What a word resolves to (kismis → raisins)
basket run-nowQueue your default list on the running service
basket sweep-nowQueue one catalogue sweep batch now
basket serve --host=0.0.0.0Start the service and let your phone reach it
