My payment methods

Your cards, UPI apps and wallets, e.g. “HDFC Credit Card” or “BHIM UPI”. Kept on this device only. They are used to pick the best payment offer for a basket; they are never sent anywhere. Only an offer's own text goes to the AI.

AI labels (optional)

Sorts saved products from sites that send no category (Blinkit, Amazon, Flipkart Minutes) into staple / packaged / sweets / pooja. A label only moves a product down the list, it never hides one. Only product titles are sent, many in one call, to the provider you pick. Batch size, pause and the daily cap are in ai.md in the data folder; edit that file to change them.
