Delivery location

Every site is searched at this address. If you change it, every site picks up the new location on the next run.

Typed into site address pickers (Zepto, BigBasket)
e.g. "Marutham Prestige Tambaram"

Default sites for manual runs

These are ticked when you open the compare page.

Default list

Scheduled runs check this list. Put one item per line, with an optional quantity in front (2x toor dal 1kg). Lines starting with # are ignored.

Schedules

Times are 24-hour . Twice a day catches the morning and evening deals on Instamart and BigBasket.

Catalogue sweep

Fills in prices for catalogue products you haven't searched yet. It runs a few products at a time at random gaps, missing ones first, and each product at most once a day.

Sweep status loads when the service is running.
